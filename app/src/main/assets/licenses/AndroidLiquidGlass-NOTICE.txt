18Duo adapts the rounded-rectangle signed-distance functions and the lens-refraction AGSL shader
(app/src/main/java/com/jake/duolauncher/LiquidGlassShaders.kt). Modifications: corner lookup in
centred coordinates, a bounded 3-tap RGB dispersion in place of the 7-tap spectral version, and a
new directional specular-rim shader. The Compose modifier that draws the glass (LiquidGlass.kt) is
an independent implementation on top of the Haze backdrop capture; no other upstream code is used.
